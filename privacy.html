ezclips.pro — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

This policy describes what ezclips.pro collects, why, and your choices.

1. What we collect

  • Account information: your email and display name, via our sign-in provider (Clerk).
  • Connected platforms: when you link Twitch or TikTok, we receive your public profile (username, avatar, platform user ID) and the access tokens needed to act on your behalf within the scopes you granted. Tokens are encrypted at rest.
  • Content you submit: VODs you ask us to process, the clips produced from them, transcripts generated for captions, and the detection signals (audio characteristics, chat activity) used to find highlights.
  • Usage and decisions: which clips you approve or reject, used to improve highlight detection quality.
  • Billing: payments are processed by Stripe; we never see or store full card details.

2. What we do with it

  • Operate the service: detect highlights, render clips, and publish them where you direct us to.
  • Improve detection quality using aggregate signals and your approve/reject decisions.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and we do not post anywhere without your authorization.

3. Retention and deletion

  • Source VOD files are retained only as long as needed for processing; rendered clips are retained per your plan's retention window.
  • Disconnecting a platform account revokes and removes its stored tokens.
  • You may request export or deletion of your account data at any time; deletion removes your personal data and content within 30 days, except records we must keep for legal or billing reasons.

4. Sharing

We share data only with the processors needed to run the service (hosting, authentication, payments, the platforms you explicitly connect) and where required by law.

5. Contact

Privacy questions or export/deletion requests: ezclips.pro@gmail.com
